Platform features

Everything working together for a better campus

Smart Campus brings campus cleanliness and resource management workflows into one connected platform.

🗑️

Smart Dustbins

View dustbin information and monitor reported fill levels to support timely waste collection.

📣

Complaint Reporting

Give students and faculty a simple way to report littering, overflowing bins and other campus concerns.

🧹

Cleaning Task Management

Help administrators organise cleaning work and coordinate task assignments with housekeeping teams.

🤖

Incidents & AI Alerts

Provide a central place to review recorded campus incidents and AI-assisted alerts.

How it works

Small actions. Meaningful impact.

The platform connects campus community members with the teams responsible for keeping shared spaces clean.

STEP 01

Notice and report

Students and faculty can sign in to report campus cleanliness issues with useful details.

STEP 02

Review and coordinate

Administrators review reports, monitor campus information and organise cleaning tasks.

STEP 03

Take action

Housekeeping teams view assigned tasks, update progress and help resolve reported issues.

About the initiative

Building a more responsible campus, together.

Smart Campus is designed for Nagarjuna College of Engineering and Technology, Devanahalli. It brings together digital reporting, task coordination and campus monitoring to support a cleaner and more sustainable campus environment.
